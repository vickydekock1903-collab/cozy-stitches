A little about me ♡

Hi, I'm the maker!

Welcome to Cozy Stitches! I'm a one-woman crochet business, turning colourful yarn and a little imagination into handmade creations.

Every item is made by me, stitch by stitch, with lots of care and love. I adore making cute characters and little pieces that bring a smile to someone's face.

Thank you for supporting my small handmade business. Every order, enquiry and kind word means so much to me! 🌷
