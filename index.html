About Me 💗

Hi, I’m Vicky, the 21-year-old creator behind Cozy Stitches — a small handmade crochet business built with love, creativity, and a whole lot of determination. 🧶🌸

I graduated from Framesby High School in 2023, and I’ve been crocheting for as long as I can remember. What started as something I simply loved doing has grown into a way for me to earn an income and support the people I love.

I’m currently trying to support my family of four: my grandmother, my mother, my younger brother and my little sister. My grandmother is 75 and, after recently falling and fracturing her hip, she is struggling to walk and needs my help with her day-to-day care. While my mom works, I help look after my grandmother and my younger siblings.

Because of my responsibilities at home, getting a regular job isn’t really an option for me right now. So I decided to turn something I’ve loved since childhood into something that can help support my family.

That’s where Cozy Stitches comes in. 💕

I’m based in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth) and run Cozy Stitches from my home. Orders can be placed conveniently via WhatsApp at +27 78 622 9197. Simply tap the number to start a WhatsApp chat with me. Since every item is handmade by me, customers can choose to collect their orders from my residential address or make use of PAXI or PUDO delivery options.

Every item I make is crocheted by hand with care and attention to detail. When you support my small business, you’re not just buying something handmade — you’re helping a young entrepreneur build something of her own while taking care of the family she loves.

Thank you for supporting Cozy Stitches and being part of my journey. 🌷🧶
